# Proficiencies
---
**Armor:** Light Armor, Medium Armor, Shields

**Weapons:** Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons

**Saving Throws:** Strength, Dexterity

**Skills:** _Choose 3:_ Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Investigation, Nature, Perception, Stealth, or Survival
# Features
---
You gain advantage on Perception checks to detect hidden foes and can also sense an Invisible creature within 60ft, allowing you to attempt Perception checks to find them. You also gain advantage on Survival checks to find tracks.
