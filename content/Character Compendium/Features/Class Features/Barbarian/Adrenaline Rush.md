**Bonus Action**

Use up one Use of Rage / or end your Rage (if you are raging) to focus on an enemy that you can see. Until the opponent is killed or you fall unconscious: 

- You are immune to disadvantage when attacking that target.
- If you kill it: you may use one hit die to heal yourself, then add the value healed to your next attack roll.
