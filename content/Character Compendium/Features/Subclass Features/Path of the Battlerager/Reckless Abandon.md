**Once per Turn**

When you use Reckless Attack, while raging, you gain Temporary Hitpoints equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1). While you have these Temporary hitpoints, an attacker has to succeed on a Wisdom Saving Throw DC 10 + current Temporary Hitpoints on a fail the attackers damage does not reach beyond the Temporary Hitpoints.

These Temporary Hit points may stack with themselves. They vanish when your rage ends.
