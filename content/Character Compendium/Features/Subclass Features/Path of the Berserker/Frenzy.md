You go into a Frenzy Rage instead of your [[Rage]]. When your rage ends, you suffer one level of Exhaustion but for the duration of your rage you gain two features:

---
##### Frenzied Attack
**Bonus Action**
Make a single melee weapon attack.
##### Frenzied Reckless
**Once per Turn**
While attacking with Reckless Attack your chosen Strength-based attack deals extra damage. The damage is (Rage Damage bonus) * d6. This damage has the same type as the weapon or Unarmed Strike used for the attack.
