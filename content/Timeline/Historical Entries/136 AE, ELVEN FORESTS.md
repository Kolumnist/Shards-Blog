A drunk halfling in some Eternian tavern:  "Those Elves thought if the dwarfs and humans have so much land, then we have to have more."

The elves forced the orcs in Orcsnet to go away. They used every trick and action. The orcs had no chance to begin with. It was not invaded, there were battles but they were kept to skirmishes at most. The orc got taken over by trickery, ressources and convincing.

The elves then officially renamed Orcsnet to Elven Forests. This hurt a lot globally.
