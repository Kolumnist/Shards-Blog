Dam Won had a good connection to the gods and travelled to Ethorn to enhance his magic however he saw the treatment of the weaker ones and decided to motivate a halfling, a tabaxi and a lizardfolk to get their friends and make their own farm land to the west of ethorn. This would land would later be called Nats. From that village some people of strong faith wanted to build a city further away from ethorn. They did and Dam Won supported them. And as his blessings were strong and potent and his connection to the gods was not just for show, the people around him changed. They werent interested in the gods anymore then they were in Dam Won himself. The king of the new city wasnt defined and many wanted Dam Won to do it. However he declined over and over. Each year passing and his glory and statues increasing.

They even called the city after him and decided to make an organization to honor his faith of both the god of darkness and the goddess of light. The people were worshipping his faith and almost himself. Around that time Dam Won disappeared forever, he was never found.

Before he vanished, it is suspected he met with the gods and ascended. However the people stopped to pray to him.
