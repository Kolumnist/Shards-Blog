The leaving dwarfs found homes in some places and one of them was to the far north of Eternum.

Eternum received a letter on this specific place. The dwarfs claimed it to be in their posession and the dwarven race will have it for as long as their race prevails!

The land they claimed was full of ice and mountains. Fitting the name Dwarven Icebergs like a glove on a hand.
