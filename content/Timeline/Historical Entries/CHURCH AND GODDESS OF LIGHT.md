#TimelineEquilibrium 

The Goddess of Light became known as the primary religious figure. Following the light of her lawful holy scripts has created this first and new religion. She was getting praised by the people and buildings like cathedrals were built to praise her even more.

---

Few have really seen the Goddess of Light but most did hear the voice of this being. The goddess was made responsible for blessing Eternum with life and greatness. Though not being seen there were many who believed they did see this god. Some say it looked like a dragon others say the sun itself and the light or colors are the god itself or maybe her creations. Most say that the Goddess of Light is not responsible for the light itself but rather for the goodness in each and everything. The cathedrals were mostly built by humans striving for accomplishments. There were no rules but one: Be a good man, do the best you can. Later they not only had some rules and laws but got instructions from the goddess herself.
