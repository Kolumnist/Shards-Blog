#TimelineEquilibrium

The first organization/group/guild Iron Hide, to support adventurers and help them find a suitable mission and party, was founded.

---

The organization was founded by dwarves in an attempt to strengthen their power in Eternum. Many who joined this organization were half-orcs, dwarves, Half-elves, and Humans striving for treasure or more. Adventurers weren't completely uncommon but before the founding, no one cared. Now it has become a promising occupation!  Even tho the dwarves really outdid themselves with this idea, they weren't as recognized as they wanted.
