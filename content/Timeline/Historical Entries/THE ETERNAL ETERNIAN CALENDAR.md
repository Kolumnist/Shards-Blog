#TimelineEquilibrium 

The first calendar, the eternal calendar was established and published in the vast city of Eternum. AE = After Eternum.

---
Time started moving for the first time! The first day was set to the foundation of Eternum. They defined seven months all having 50 days. This time template got defined to honor the seven important races that founded Eternum after 50 years. The Months and their race:

1. Progress -> Humans
2. Dignitas -> Elves
3. Spaß -> Halflings
4. Mythos -> Tieflings
5. Dracos -> Dragonborn
6. Pathos -> Lizardfolk
7. Inizine -> Tabaxi

Two important races for the foundation of Eternum weren't mentioned in this publishing: The Dwarves, who helped build a LOT like A LOT and were gathering resources underground. The Orcs were hard workers and defended the civilization and its people. These two races were fuming with anger. So angry that even some of them decided to leave Eternum
